1. About the app

Rozgar Mitra for VB-G RAM G makes the public information of the Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin), VB-G RAM G, easier to read and understand in your own language. It shows job card, wage, payment and work demand information as simple lists and PDFs. It only brings information that the Government has already made public into one place. It is made in good faith and is not meant to mislead anyone. For any official decision, certificate or payment, please check the official portal or your Gram Panchayat office.

2. Sources of the information shown

All government information shown in the app is public information from these official websites of the Department of Rural Development, Government of India. The app does not log in to these websites with any user name or password. It reads only public pages that anyone can open:

  • VB-G RAM G portal: https://vbgramg.dord.gov.in
  • VB-G RAM G MIS reports: https://vbgramgrep.dord.gov.in/VBGRAMG/MISreport.aspx
  • NREGA portal (earlier scheme, MGNREGA): https://nrega.dord.gov.in/Nregahome/MGNREGA_new/Nrega_home.aspx

These official websites remain the only source of truth. The app does not create or change any government record.

3. Information we collect

  • Sign-in (Google account): To open the app as an Officer or as a Wage seeker, you may sign in with your Google account. We receive your name and e-mail address through Firebase Authentication (a Google service). This is used only to let you in and to show your name in the app.
  • Information you type: For example your name and designation, the Gram Panchayat you choose, mate group lists, attendance, targets and letters you prepare. This stays on your phone.
  • Public portal information: Job card, member, payment, attendance and work information read from the public websites above, and member photos shown on those pages, are saved on your phone so that you can see them again without waiting.
  • Images you choose: A signature and seal image, if you choose to add them for identity cards and letters, are saved on your phone.
  • The app does not ask for passwords, Aadhaar numbers or bank account numbers, and does not use advertising.

4. Where your data is kept

Saved data, backup files and PDFs stay on your phone. We do not run a server that receives your data. The only ways information leaves your phone are: (a) the Google sign-in described above, (b) when you yourself print, share or save a PDF or backup file, and (c) the normal requests your phone makes to the public websites listed in section 2 and to Google (for sign-in and text-to-speech).

5. Permissions used

  • Internet: to open the public websites and for sign-in.
  • Notifications: for the optional daily reminder to save attendance. You can switch it off in the app.
  • Files / photos you pick: only when you choose a signature, seal image or backup file.

6. Voice guide

The voice guide uses your phone's own text-to-speech. We do not record your voice.

7. We do not sell or share your data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone. There are no advertisements and no analytics that identify you.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults: workers, Gram Panchayat staff and officers. It is not directed at children.

9. Deleting your data

  • All data saved by the app is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in the phone settings, removes it.
  • To delete the Google sign-in information (name and e-mail) held by Firebase Authentication for your account, send a request from your own e-mail address to burrolamurali@gmail.com. We will delete it.

10. Limits of the information

  • Information can be incomplete or out of date if the official portals are slow, changed or unavailable.
  • Translations are provided for convenience and may contain mistakes. Please verify important details on the official websites.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
