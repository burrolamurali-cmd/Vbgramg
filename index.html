Privacy Policy

App: VB-G RAM G Job Card Helper (package: com.gpdyagadoddy.vbgramg)
Developer: B Murali, Panchayat Secretary  |  Last updated: 11 October 2026
In short: This app does not collect your personal information. It has no login, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app saves stays on your own phone. The developer never receives your data.

1. About the app

VB-G RAM G Job Card Helper (“the app”) is an independent helper app. It helps users read, organise and print information that is publicly available on the official NREGA / VB-G RAM G web portal, and shows it in the user’s own language.

The app is not an official app of the Government of India or of any State or Union Territory Government. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any government body. The official portal remains the only source of truth.

2. Information we collect

The developer does not collect, receive, transmit to any server, sell, rent or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • There are no user accounts, sign-up or login. Anyone can open the app and use it directly.
  • The app contains no advertising and no advertising ID is used.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools from any third party.
  • The app does not ask for your name, phone number, e-mail address, Aadhaar number, bank details, password or location.

3. Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves some information only on your own phone:

  • Your language choice and the State, District, Block and Gram Panchayat names you select or type.
  • Lists and details read from the public portal pages you open (for example job card lists, work and payment information), so you can view them again without re-loading.
  • PDF files that the app creates for you.
  • Images you choose yourself, such as a signature, seal or emblem photo, which are used when printing cards or letters.

This information never leaves your phone through the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your phone settings (Settings → Apps → VB-G RAM G Job Card Helper → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.

4. The official portal shown inside the app

The app displays the official public portal inside the app. When you choose a State, District, Block or Gram Panchayat, or press buttons on those pages, that information is sent to the official portal in the same way as when you use its website in a normal browser. The app only helps to read the page that the portal returns. We do not operate or control the portal, and we do not receive a copy of what you do there. Please refer to the portal’s own policies to learn how it handles information.

5. Printing, saving and sharing

PDF files created by the app are saved on your phone. If you decide to print, save or send a PDF to someone (for example using your phone’s print or share options), that is your own action and your own choice. The app never sends any file anywhere by itself.

6. Permissions

The app uses only the Internet permission, which is needed to load the public portal pages. It does not request access to your contacts, SMS, camera, microphone, location or phone calls. When you choose a photo (such as a signature), you pick it yourself using the phone’s standard file or photo chooser.

7. Children

The app is meant for adults such as Panchayat Secretaries, Field Assistants, officials, mates and workers. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Security

Because the developer does not receive or store your data, there is no developer database that could be exposed. Data saved on your phone is protected by your phone’s own security (screen lock, device encryption). Pages from the portal are loaded over a secure (HTTPS) connection where the portal supports it.

9. Accuracy and responsibility

Information shown in the app is read from public pages and may be incomplete, delayed or out of date, for example when the portal is slow, changed or unavailable. Translations are provided for convenience and may contain mistakes. Please verify important details on the official portal before taking any decision or action.

10. Your rights and choices

  • You can use the app without giving any personal information.
  • You can delete all data saved by the app at any time (see Section 3).
  • Since the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or delete on the developer’s side.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same web address with a new “Last updated” date.
