Privacy Policy – VB-G RAM G Job Card Helper

Last updated: 15 October 2026  |  తెలుగులో చదవండి ↓

In short: The app asks you to sign in with your Google account (Gmail) so that it can tell an Officer from a Citizen. Only your Google name and email address are used for this. Job card, payment and attendance information is kept only on your own phone and is not sent to us.

1. Who we are

"VB-G RAM G Job Card Helper" (package com.gpdyagadoddy.vbgramg) is an Android app made by B Murali, Dyagadoddi Gram Panchayat, Dharur mandal, Jogulamba Gadwal district, Telangana, India. It helps Gram Panchayat staff and citizens view job card, payment and attendance information published on the public VB-G RAM G portal. It is an independent app and is not the official government portal.

Contact: burrolamurali@gmail.com

2. What we collect

  • Google account name and email address – collected when you tap "Login as Officer" or "Login as Citizen" and choose your Google account. This is handled by Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication (Google LLC).
  • On-device settings – your chosen language, state, district, mandal and Gram Panchayat, and (for officers) the designation you type. These stay on your phone.

3. What stays on your phone

Job card lists, member names, payment and attendance details that the app reads from the public portal, any photos shown on the portal, and any PDFs you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive, upload or store them on any server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

4. How we use the information

  • Your Google name and email are used only to sign you in and to show who is signed in.
  • The app remembers on the phone that someone has signed in as a Citizen, so that the Officer sign-in is not offered on that phone.
  • We do not sell your information, do not show advertisements and do not use it for profiling.

5. Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone. Sign-in is processed by Google (Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication), under Google's Privacy Policy. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

6. Public portal

When you ask the app to fetch information, it opens the public government portal inside the app and reads the pages that portal publishes. That information is governed by the portal's own terms and privacy policy.

7. Deleting your data and account

To have your sign-in record (Google name and email held by Firebase Authentication) deleted, write to burrolamurali@gmail.com from the same email address, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days. Data on your phone can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
